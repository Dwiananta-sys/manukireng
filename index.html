About Me

I am a multimedia-based creative professional with experience in video editing, motion graphics, photography, graphic design, and digital content production. I began my journey in vocational school focusing on multimedia and continued developing my skills through university and organizational involvement.

I have experience working as a video editor and software educator within a university organization, where I not only handled technical production but also guided others in understanding creative workflows. I also served as Vice Chairman of a national multimedia event, strengthening my leadership, coordination, and project management skills under real deadlines.

I am capable of handling digital content end-to-end — from concept and planning, production (photo and video), editing, motion graphics, design, to publication. I consider myself adaptable, detail-oriented, and comfortable working both independently and within a team environment.
